A Sustainable World

🎯 GOAL: Increase your SCORE to win!

Score is your primary objective. Sustainability helps but Score wins the game.

How to Increase Score

  • 💀 Kill Players: +5 Score
  • 🌳 Harvest Grown Trees: +5 Score
  • 🌲 Collect Wild Trees: +3 Score
  • ⚡ Build Solar Panels: +3 Score
  • 🌱 Plant Trees: +2 Score

Complete Resource Guide

🌳 Trees (Green - Walk Over to Collect)

  • What it gives: +1 Sustainability, +3 Score, +3 Ammo
  • Why collect: +3 Score! Quick way to increase your score
  • Strategy: Collect wild trees for immediate score, plant your own for long-term gains

💎 Minerals (Orange/Gold Octahedrons - Walk Over to Collect)

  • What it gives: +1 Mineral (max 50)
  • Why collect: Build solar panels! Need 5 minerals per panel
  • How to use: Collect 5 minerals → Press R to build a solar panel
  • Score reward: Building a panel gives +3 Score!
  • Strategy: Build panels early - they give score AND generate energy for abilities

🌊 Flowing Water System

  • How it works: Water flows naturally across the terrain, collecting in low areas (rivers and pools)
  • Drinking: Stand near flowing water to automatically drink - water drains from the area as you drink
  • Water decay: Your water decays 0.1/second when not near water
  • Danger: If water hits 0, you lose 0.2 health/second until death!
  • Strategy: Follow water flow to find pools in low areas - water flows downhill to local minimums
  • Rain system: When global water levels get too low, it will rain to replenish water across the map

🌱 Seeds (Brown Spheres - Walk Over to Collect)

  • What it gives: +1 Seed (max 10)
  • Why collect: Plant trees to earn score!
  • How to use: Press E to plant a tree (uses 1 seed)
  • Planting reward: +2 Score immediately when you plant!
  • Growth time: 30 seconds to fully grow (3 stages)
  • Harvest reward: +2 Sustainability, +5 Score, +1 Seed (you get the seed back!)
  • Enhanced planting: Press Shift+E to use 10 energy for instant full growth
  • Strategy: Plant trees early - get +2 score immediately, then +5 more when you harvest!

⚡ Energy System (Generated by Solar Panels)

  • How to get: Build solar panels with minerals (5 minerals = 1 panel)
  • Generation: Each panel generates 0.5 energy/second (max 100 energy)
  • More panels = faster energy: 2 panels = 1 energy/sec, 4 panels = 2 energy/sec
  • Uses for energy:
    • Q - Speed Boost: 20 energy, 5 seconds, 1.5x speed (escape/chase)
    • F - Shield: 30 energy, 10 seconds, blocks 1 bullet (survival)
    • G - Resource Scanner: 15 energy, 10 seconds, highlights resources within 200m (find resources fast!)
    • T - Teleport: 25 energy, teleport 5 units in movement direction (tactical positioning)
    • Shift+E - Enhanced Plant: 10 energy, instant tree growth (quick sustainability)
  • Strategy: Build 4-6 panels early for steady energy income, then use abilities strategically

🎯 Winning Strategy - Maximize Your Score!

  • Early game: Collect trees (+3 score each) → Collect minerals → Build solar panels (+3 score each)
  • Mid game: Plant trees (+2 score each) → Collect water to survive → Use scanner to find resources
  • Late game: Harvest grown trees (+5 score each) → Hunt players (+5 score per kill) → Build more panels
  • Key tip: Score is everything! Every action should aim to increase your score. Sustainability is secondary.

Controls: Click to lock pointer. WASD to move, mouse to look, left-click to shoot.
